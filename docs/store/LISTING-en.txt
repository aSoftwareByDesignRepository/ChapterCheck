ChapterCheck

--- Short description (≈ 1 line / AppStream <summary>) ---
Local audiobook and music player for Linux with resume, speed, and sleep timer.

--- Full description ---
ChapterCheck is a quiet desktop player for long-form audio on Linux. Link your own folders, continue where you left off, change speed, and set a sleep timer — without a streaming account.

SEARCHING FOR
Linux audiobook player, local music player, mpv front-end, resume playback SQLite, offline Hörbuch player, sleep timer audiobook, folder playlist Linux.

WHO IT IS FOR
• People who keep audiobooks and music as files on disk
• Listeners who want resume and speed without cloud lock-in
• Linux Mint / Ubuntu users who prefer a simple .deb or Flatpak

WHAT YOU NEED
• Linux desktop (x86_64)
• mpv on PATH (bundled as a package dependency for .deb)
• Folders or files you own (no hosted demo library)

WHAT THE APP DOES
• Link library folders once — scan audiobooks and music collections
• Resume progress in a local SQLite library under your user profile
• Variable playback speed and a process-level sleep timer
• Playlists, continue listening, and hardware media keys (MPRIS)
• Optional online cover/title lookup (Open Library / MusicBrainz) — off until you allow it
• Playback engine: mpv

PRIVACY
• No ads, no analytics SDKs
• Library paths and progress stay on your machine
• Optional metadata lookup only after an explicit OS confirm dialog
• See the privacy policy URL in the store listing
